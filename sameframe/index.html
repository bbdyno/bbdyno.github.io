Sameframe

A filming assistant for two iPhones. No server, no account — the two iPhones find each other on the same Wi-Fi, share one clock, and work together.

Tally Light

Tap a cue and the other iPhone's screen lights up at the same moment.

Delayed Playback

Watch the camera view on the other iPhone a few seconds later. Rewind, slow motion and drawing for coaching.

Wireless Mic Recording

One iPhone films, the other is a microphone in a pocket. The audio is aligned automatically into one finished video.
